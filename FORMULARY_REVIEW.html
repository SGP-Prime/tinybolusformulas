About this document

This is the human-readable export of the TinyBolus paediatric reference formulary, organised section-by-section as it appears in the application. Each item is presented with its dose specification, applicable age brackets (where present), route, clinical notes, and numerical references pointing into the bibliography at the end of the document.

How to read each item

Items fall into a small number of shapes that map directly onto how the application computes them:

  • Single dose (e.g., 10 µg/kg): one factor multiplied by weight, often with a hard maximum cap.
  • Dose range (e.g., 2–3 mg/kg): a low–high range, also weight-scaled, optionally with a maximum cap on either bound.
  • Age-bracketed: different dose factors for different age cohorts. Rendered as a small table per item.
  • Age-bracketed fixed value: not weight-scaled, just a string per age band (e.g., laryngoscope blade size).
  • Computed: piecewise logic that doesn't reduce to a single formula (ETT sizing, fluid maintenance, LMA / supraglottic airway sizing, vital-sign normal ranges).

Age brackets

Bracket bounds are printed exactly as the application resolves them. A bound in months is inclusive in completed months ("≤12 mo" includes a child of exactly 1 y 0 m). A bound in years covers every child of that age in completed years ("1–6 y" runs through 6 y 11 m). Where a months bracket ends on a birthday, the next bracket starts one month later and is marked ">": ">1–6 y" begins at 1 y 1 m. "≥6 mo" starts at exactly 6 months. Weight bands are inclusive on both bounds.

Vital signs

The four vital-sign rows are not bracketed. They carry the ERC Paediatric Life Support 2025 reference values (Table 2) at six anchor ages — 1 mo, 1 y, 2 y, 5 y, 10 y, 18 y — and the app interpolates linearly between the two nearest anchors for the patient's exact age, as the guideline instructs ("use intermediate values for children between the specified ages"). Below 1 month the 1-month column is used and the row says so. Blood pressure shows the 5th-to-50th percentile band (hypotension line to median) with the 10th percentile — ERC's post-resuscitation target — on the formula line; percentiles are for the 50th height percentile.

Visibility

This document prints every row in the formulary. The app shows a curated default set and keeps the remaining rows behind a switch in Settings; those rows are tagged hidden by default beside their name below. A hidden row is still maintained, sourced and reviewed — it is simply off until a user enables it.

Groups

A long section may carry sub-headings (Emergency Drugs: Anaphylaxis, Respiratory, Cardiovascular, Metabolic, Seizures, Malignant hyperthermia, Intracranial pressure). They are labels only — every row keeps its own identity, visibility switch and citations — and the app prints a label above the first visible row of its group.

Hard floors

Some items carry an age or weight floor below which the formulary declines to suggest a dose. In the app this renders as ∅ (empty-set glyph) with a "not indicated" explanation; the same wording is used here. A floor records where the cited source stops (no data, not recommended, outside the licensed indication) — it is not a claim of formal contraindication. Items with such floors are flagged in red below each row.

References

Every dose / range / age bracket / sizing rule carries one or more citations. References are numbered in encounter order throughout this document, with a full bibliography at the end. The numbers in [brackets] after each item refer to entries in that bibliography.

Source

The formulary itself lives at github.com/SGP-Prime/tinybolusformulas as a single machine-readable JSON file. This document is regenerated from that file on demand by tool/generate_formulary_review_html.dart; do not hand-edit it.
